Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers the Hej Odisha publishing tool ("the app"), a private tool used by the owner of the Hej Odisha (@hejodisha) channel to manage the channel's own content.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the Hej Odisha channel owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app may access the owner's own Google account data needed for publishing work, such as files in the owner's Google Drive and the owner's YouTube channel. It only accesses the scopes the owner approves on the Google consent screen.

How the data is used

Data is used only to create, organise and publish Hej Odisha content. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored securely and used only by the app. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
